# Video script: what a weighted average is (about 90 seconds)

*For the knowledge check on the student site. Plain voice, one example, no Census table codes. On-screen cues are in brackets.*

**[Title card: "The average member of your group"]**

Say you want to know what the neighborhood is like for the typical member of an immigrant group. You collect one number for each city, like the poverty rate. The easy move is to add them up and divide by the number of cities. That is the **plain average**. It treats every city the same.

**[Show three cities in a table]**

| City | Group members | Poverty rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 100 | 10% |
| B | 100 | 20% |
| C | 800 | 30% |

The plain average of the three rates is 20%. But eight out of every ten people in this group live in city C. A typical member does not live in a city at 20%. They live in a city at 30%.

**[Animate: each city's rate multiplied by its group size]**

The fix is a **weighted average**. Give each city a weight equal to how many group members live there. Multiply each city's rate by its weight. Add those up: 1,000, plus 2,000, plus 24,000, makes 27,000. Add up the weights: 1,000. Divide: **27%**.

**[Show: weighted average = sum of (weight × value) ÷ sum of weights]**

That is the whole idea: the weighted average describes the average **member**, and the plain average describes the average **city**. When the group is spread unevenly, they can be far apart.

**[Show the Weighted averages rows for the same three cities (1,000 / 27,000 / 27%), then say that a real page has fifty cities]**

On your page, each number is built exactly this way. Two rows, and one division. Your page has fifty cities, not three, so its numbers are bigger, but the rows are the same. Find them, check them, and you can explain where every number came from.

**[Closing card: "Now try the questions."]**

---

*Notes for whoever records it:* keep the table on screen while the sums are spoken; slow down on "27,000 over 1,000"; no jargon beyond "weight". If you want a real example afterward, use a group from the site and show the city that carries the most weight.
