# Week 12. Place and access

## Goals
- Describe how nearby features (hospitals, housing, green space, air quality) relate to neighborhood outcomes.
- Measure and compare distance and access with the tools available.
- Think about place-based interventions.

## Concept (25 min)
- Environmental criminology: routine activity theory, crime pattern theory, broken windows and its critiques, greening and vacant-lot studies.
- Access: Euclidean distance vs travel time. Trauma center distance and survival is a public health link to violence.
- Anchor places: schools, transit, bars, housing.

## Lab (50 min)
1. Turn on trauma centers, public housing and green space for one city. Describe the spatial relationships you see.
2. Use HEV's routing (where available) to find travel time from three neighborhoods to the nearest trauma center.
3. Compare green space and violence at the tract level, using a merged file from week 8.
4. Add air quality. Which neighborhoods carry several burdens at once?
5. Annotate one place-based intervention and list what you would measure to evaluate it.

## Discussion
If green space and lower violence go together, what would you need to show before
recommending that a city plant trees?

## Deliverable
Access comparison table for three neighborhoods and a one-page intervention memo.

## ArcGIS Pro equivalent
Near, Buffer, Network Analyst (Service Areas, Closest Facility).

## Gap note
No buffer or service-area tool. Students compare distances and routes by hand
and note what a network analysis would add.
